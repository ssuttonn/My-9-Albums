#My9Albums

How it works

Add
Click any empty + square, search for or an artist, and pick one. The cover fills the square.
Listen
Click a cover to hear a 30-second preview of . Click it again to stop.
Rearrange
Press and drag a cover onto another one to swap them, or onto an empty square to move it. It works with a finger too.
Change or remove
Hover over a cover. The ⇄ button picks a different for that spot, and the × clears it.
The description line
The line under each cover is for a short note of your own, like why it mattered to you. Whatever you write there shows up in your saved image.
Make it your own
Click the title or the line under it and type whatever you like. Each of Albums and Songs remembers its own wording. If you're not sure what to write, tap an idea to use it as your title:
The buttons at the bottom
Albums / Songs switches between your two lists, and each keeps its own picks. Copy link makes a link to your collage you can send to someone. Theme changes the colors. Generate image saves your collage as a picture.
